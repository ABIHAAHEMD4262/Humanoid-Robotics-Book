# Module 0 – Foundations of Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence

Welcome to Module 0, where we lay the groundwork for understanding Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence. This foundational module introduces the core concepts, definitions, and key differences between traditional AI and Physical AI.

## What You'll Learn

In this module, we will cover:

- **The Evolution from Digital to Physical AI**: Understanding the shift from screen-based AI to embodied systems
- **Core Concepts in Robotics**: Essential terminology and principles
- **The Sense-Think-Act Cycle**: How robots perceive, process, and respond to their environment
- **Key Components of Physical AI Systems**: Sensors, actuators, compute platforms, and software stacks
- **Real-World Applications**: Current use cases and future possibilities

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. Define Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence
2. Explain the fundamental differences between digital AI and Physical AI
3. Identify the key components of a Physical AI system
4. Understand the Sense-Think-Act cycle in robotics
5. Recognize current applications and limitations of Physical AI

## Prerequisites

This module assumes:
- Basic familiarity with AI/ML concepts (though we'll review key points)
- Comfort with technical reading
- Curiosity about robotics and embodied systems

Let's begin our journey into the fascinating world of Physical AI!
